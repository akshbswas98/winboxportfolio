about-me:$

I build AI systems, and I’m deliberately moving toward the role where the AI meets the customer’s production reality: Forward Deployed Engineering.

The draw for me is migration work — the part most teams treat as a tax. Standing up a model is a solved problem; moving an existing system onto new infrastructure without dropping a request is not. It means reading a codebase nobody documented, mapping every dependency, staging the cutover, and having a rollback that you have actually rehearsed. I treat “zero downtime, zero regressions” as an engineering constraint, not an aspiration.

What I optimise for

  • Zero-downtime cutovers — dual-write, backfill, flip, verify.
  • Rehearsed rollback — a plan nobody has tested is not a plan.
  • Observability before migration — you cannot trust what you cannot measure.
  • Boring final weeks — the old system should be uneventful.

This site is hand-rolled vanilla JS on WinBox — no framework, no build step.
